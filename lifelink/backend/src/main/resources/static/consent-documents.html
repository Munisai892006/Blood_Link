4-Doctor Medical Board Certifications

Under THOTA Act 1994, cadaveric brain death requires two independent evaluations at least 6 hours apart by a board of 4 authorized doctors:

1st Evaluation: Form 8 CERTIFIED
Examined: Apnea test target pCO2 > 60 mmHg reached, absence of brainstem reflexes confirmed.
2nd Evaluation: Form 10 CERTIFIED
Examined 6 hours post-first test: Irreversible cessation of brainstem function confirmed.
