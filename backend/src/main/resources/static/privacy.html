Privacy & Medical Data Protection Policy

Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 & Indian Medical Council Regulations

1. Anonymized Clinical Telemetry

LifeLink coordinates cadaveric organ transport and clinical evaluations under strict statutory anonymity. Public dashboards display only institutional hospital codes and anonymous tracking numbers (e.g. TRP-2026-001).

2. Zero-Knowledge Financial Security

LifeLink NEVER collects, requests, stores, or handles user UPI PINs, banking passwords, or card CVVs. All contributions are processed through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment gateways with end-to-end TLS encryption.

3. Statutory Data Retention

Under the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Rules, all Form 6 consent documents, Form 8/10 brain death board certifications, and police MLC clearances are retained securely for a minimum statutory period of ten (10) years.
