Strict Prohibition of Commercial Dealings in Human Organs

Non-Commercial Organ Mandate (THOTA 1994)

Section 18 of the Transplantation of Human Organs and Tissues Act, 1994 makes any commercial transaction, buying, selling, or offering of financial reward for human organs a cognizable criminal offence punishable with rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years and fines up to ₹1 Crore.

1. Clinical Boundaries & Non-Commercial Logistics

LifeLink is an administrative, communication, and transport telemetry coordination tool. LifeLink does not perform algorithmic organ matching or commercial allocations; all medical matching rests solely with authorized government nodal centers (NOTTO/ROTTO/SOTTO) and licensed hospital transplant teams.

2. Transparent Logistics Contributions (Section 80G)

All monetary contributions received on LifeLink are dedicated exclusively to operational equipment (sterile cold preservation chambers, perfusion machine rental, ambulance transit fuel, and statutory green corridor police escort fees). Donors receive genuine 80G tax exemption receipts verified with SHA-256 digital integrity hashes.
